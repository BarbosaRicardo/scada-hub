## ⚡ Validated verdict (human-checked against page content)

**Real content gaps — survey material with no home in any guide:**
1. **NGVL** (RTAC AUTO Wk3) — zero mentions anywhere in the SEL RTAC guide. The survey has a whole P2P category for it. Needs a section in Ch 7 (Protocol Gateway) or the Lab.
2. **CFC language** (RTAC AUTO Wk4) — the IEC 61131-3 guide teaches FBD but never names CFC, which is what the survey (and ACSELERATOR) actually calls it. Needs a "CFC = SEL's free-form FBD" treatment in Ch 5.
3. **Tag Splitter** (SCADA OPS Wk5) — zero mentions in the Ignition guide.
4. **Bulk-editing tags** (SCADA OPS Wk3) — zero mentions in the Ignition guide.
5. **Programmable Device Simulator** (SCADA OPS Wk2) — one passing mention; pre-flagged on the hub as a gap. Still the thinnest Wk2 item.

**Terminology misalignment (content exists, survey vocabulary doesn't):**
- **"P2P"** — the survey uses this term in 10+ competencies; the guides say "point-to-point" once and otherwise describe the activity without naming it. Cheap, high-value fix: use the survey's exact term in every troubleshooting/checkout section.
- **"MirroredBits"** — written as "mirrored bits" in the RTAC guide; align spelling with SEL/survey usage.

**Keyword-screen noise (verified fine, no action):** rosters/escalation (present in Ignition alarming),
import/export XML (present in RTAC), authentication concepts, ST/LD coverage, store-and-forward recovery.
